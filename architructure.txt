     |

YOLO Vehicle Detection
     |
     ↓

     |

Vehicle IDs
     |
     |
     ├───────────────┐
     ↓               ↓
Vehicle Box     YOLO Plate Detection
                         |
                         ↓
                 Plate Bounding Box
                         |
                         ↓
                  IoU Matching
                         |
                         ↓
              Vehicle ID + Plate Number
                         |
                         ↓
                    Database

So the matching is basically geometry + tracking, not AI understanding.
